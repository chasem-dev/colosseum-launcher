Frame rate

Sets everything under Quality below. Change any of them and the preset becomes Custom.

Still seeing frame drops? Try a lower screen resolution: under Display, set Fullscreen type to Exclusive and choose a lower Fullscreen resolution.

Quality

FSR 1 (AMD) and NIS (NVIDIA Image Scaling) render below your screen's resolution and upscale with clean edges: Quality at 67%, Balanced 59%, Performance 50%, Ultra Performance 33%, Native at full size. They set the resolution themselves, so Picture sharpness is set aside while one is on. Sharpening sets how crisp they are. Both work on any graphics card.
